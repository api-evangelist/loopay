# Loopay

> Loopay is a Latin American B2B fintech infrastructure company (Bogotá, Colombia; 500 Global-backed) offering cross-border and local business payments, international collections, mass payouts, multi-currency Banking-as-a-Service accounts, automated bank reconciliation, and treasury management. Its token-authenticated REST API (OpenAPI 3.0, 31 operations) lets platforms create payouts, manage sources of payment, and read balances/movements across Colombia, Peru, Mexico, and Ecuador.

## Specs
- [OpenAPI (harvested)](openapi/loopay-openapi-original.json): 31 operations across Payout, PayIn, SourcesOfPayment, Affiliate, Company, Movements, and reference-data tags
- [Authentication](authentication/loopay-authentication.yml): token-based bearer auth via POST /login
- [Conventions](conventions/loopay-conventions.yml): pagination (page/recordsPerPage), external-identifier reconciliation, no idempotency contract
- [Data model](data-model/loopay-data-model.yml): CompanyProduct-centric entity graph
- [Conformance](conformance/loopay-conformance.yml): REST/OpenAPI; no OAuth2/OIDC/RFC9457
- [MCP (candidate)](mcp/loopay-mcp.yml): candidate tool list derived from operations
